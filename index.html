Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a Life Path Number?

The Life Path Number is a symbolic number in numerology calculated from your birth date, representing core personality themes and reflective prompts.

How is the Life Path Number calculated?

It is calculated by reducing the month, day, and year of your birth separately to single digits, and then summing and reducing those numbers to a final digit or Master Number (11, 22, 33).
